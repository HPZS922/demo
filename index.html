🔐 Public Wi-Fi Safety

Remember these five rules when using public or unfamiliar networks.

📶 Verify the networkConfirm the official Wi-Fi name with the business, school, or venue.
🌐 Check the websiteLook at the real domain and use HTTPS. Don't trust a padlock alone.
🛡️ Understand VPNsA VPN can protect traffic to the VPN service, but it does not make you completely safe or anonymous.
📂 Disable sharingTurn off unnecessary file sharing and network discovery on unfamiliar networks.
🚫 Think before sensitive actionsBe extra cautious with passwords, banking, and other sensitive activity.
🧠 Don't panicUnexpected warnings and urgent messages may be designed to make you react without checking.
